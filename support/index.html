FitWheel Support Help, questions and contact for the FitWheel iOS app. Last updated: 4 September 2026 FitWheel is an augmented-reality app that lets you preview wheels and brake kits on your own car before you buy. It is published by Cengine. If something is not working, or you have a question this page does not answer, email us — a real person reads it. Contact: cengine.fitwheel@gmail.com We aim to reply within a few business days. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps us answer faster. Getting started Placing a wheel Point your camera at the ground near your car and move the phone slowly from side to side so the app can find the surface. When a surface is detected, tap to place a wheel. Good, even lighting and a bit of texture on the ground both help — plain, glossy or very dark surfaces are harder to detect. Changing rims and brakes Swipe the carousel at the bottom to try a different rim. Switch to the brake carousel to change the brake kit behind it. Changes apply to the wheel you have placed, live. Saving a build Press and hold the shutter to freeze the shot and enter edit mode, then save. Saving needs an account — that is what backs the build up and syncs it between your devices. Accounts Do I need an account? No. You can open the app, place wheels and try every rim and brake as a guest. An account is only required when you save a build. How do I sign in? FitWheel uses Sign in with Apple and Google. There is no password to create or remember — we never see or store one. How do I delete my account? In the app, open the options menu and choose Delete Account, then confirm. This permanently removes your account, your saved builds and any photos attached to them from our servers, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple token. It cannot be undone. If you would rather we did it for you, email cengine.fitwheel@gmail.com from the address on your account. Troubleshooting The camera view is black, or no surfaces are found Check that FitWheel has camera permission in Settings → FitWheel → Camera. If it does, try a brighter, more textured floor and move the phone slowly — AR needs to see the surface from a couple of angles before it can lock on. My saved builds are missing Builds saved while signed in are restored when you sign back in with the same provider. Builds saved as a guest live only on that device, so deleting the app removes them. Which devices are supported? FitWheel needs an iPhone that supports ARKit, running iOS 13 or later. Buying wheels FitWheel does not sell wheels and does not take payment. The app is a visualiser: it shows you how a set would look on your car. Retailer links will arrive in a later version. Privacy What the app collects and why is set out in full in our Privacy Policy. In short: no ads, no tracking across other companies' apps, and the camera feed is processed on your device. FitWheel is a product of Cengine. This page covers the FitWheel iOS application only.
