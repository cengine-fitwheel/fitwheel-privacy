FitWheel Privacy Policy

How FitWheel handles your information.

Effective date: 21 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 21 August 2026

FitWheel is an augmented-reality app that lets you preview wheels and brake kits on your car. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what control you have over it. It applies to the FitWheel iOS app published by Cengine ("we", "us").

The short version: You can use FitWheel without an account. We do not sell your data, we do not use advertising networks, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The camera feed used for AR is processed on your device and is never streamed to us — a photo only leaves your phone if you save a build while signed in.

1. Information we collect

a. Anonymous usage data (collected from everyone, including guests)

When you first open FitWheel, the app generates a random installation ID — a UUID stored on your device. It is not your name, your Apple ID, your device's advertising identifier, or any identifier shared with other apps. It exists so we can count how many distinct people use a feature rather than how many taps happen.

Each meaningful action records an event containing:

  • the action name (for example: app opened, wheel placed, build saved, item selected);
  • the wheel or brake product involved (its SKU and brand);
  • where in the app the action happened (live AR, edit mode, saved-build mode);
  • an internal interest score used to distinguish casual browsing from shopping intent;
  • the installation ID — and not your account, name or email. Usage events are recorded against the anonymous installation ID only, and cannot be traced back to your account.

b. Vehicle information you enter

If you tell FitWheel what car you drive, we store the make, model and generation you selected (for example: BMW / 3 Series / G20). This is used to show correct fitment and to understand which vehicles our users own. It is saved on your device and included with the usage events described above. We do not collect your licence plate, VIN, mileage, or any other vehicle identifier.

c. Account information (only if you choose to sign in)

Signing in is optional and is only needed to sync and back up your saved builds. FitWheel offers sign-in with Apple, Google, and (where enabled) Facebook. When you sign in, the provider gives us:

  • your email address (if you use Sign in with Apple's Hide My Email option, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address);
  • your name, if the provider supplies it;
  • a unique account ID issued by our authentication service.

We never receive your password. Your session token is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.

d. Saved builds and photos

When you save a build, FitWheel stores a still photo captured from your camera (the frozen background of your AR scene, which typically shows your car and its surroundings), a small thumbnail, the wheel or brake you chose, its size and offset, and where each item was placed in the scene.

  • If you are not signed in, saved builds stay on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.
  • If you are signed in, saved builds — including the photo — are uploaded to your private account so they are available if you reinstall the app or change phones. They are stored privately and are not visible to other users.

e. Camera

FitWheel requires camera access to place virtual wheels on a real car. The live camera feed is processed entirely on your device by Apple's ARKit and is never transmitted to us or stored, except for the still photo saved as part of a build, as described above.

2. What we do not collect

  • No location data. FitWheel does not request or use GPS or any location permission.
  • No health, fitness, or financial data.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or photo-library reading. (Saving an image to your camera roll writes only the image you asked to save.)
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no advertising SDKs. FitWheel does not show ads and does not participate in ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which hosts the database, authentication, and file storage where accounts, usage events, and saved builds are kept. (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Apple, Google, and Facebook — only if you choose to sign in with them. They handle the sign-in itself under their own privacy policies; we receive only the account details listed in section 1(c).

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

If we report demand to wheel brands or retailers, we do so in aggregate — for example, "42 people with a G20 3-Series viewed this wheel this month." Individual users are not identified, and your photos, email address, and name are never shared.

5. International transfers

Our backend infrastructure is operated by Supabase and may store and process data on servers located outside your country of residence. Where data is transferred out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Saved builds and photos — until you delete the build or your account. Deleting a build in the app also removes its cloud copy and its photo.
  • Account information — until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Usage events — retained for up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or aggregated. They are tied to the anonymous installation ID, never to your account.
  • Data on your device — removed when you delete the app.

7. Your choices and rights

Regardless of where you live, you can:

  • Use the app anonymously — sign-in is optional; guest builds never leave your device.
  • Revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings → FitWheel. AR features will stop working, but the app will not attempt to collect camera data.
  • Delete individual builds in the app, which deletes the cloud copy and photo too.
  • Sign out at any time.
  • Delete your account and all associated data — use Delete Account in the app's options menu. This permanently deletes your account, your saved builds and their photos, and revokes the app's access to your Apple ID if you signed in with Apple. You can also email us at the address below and we will do it for you within 30 days. Usage events are not linked to your account, so there is nothing in them to trace back to you.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data from your device.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

If you are in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion or correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

If you are in Thailand, you have the rights granted under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email cengine.fitwheel@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity — usually by confirming the email address on your account — before acting on a request.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest is protected by our backend provider's encryption and by database access rules that restrict each account's builds and photos to that account. Session tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold.

9. Children

FitWheel is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will give notice in the app before the change takes effect.
